# CheckMyDrive for Android: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026
Publisher: XronAI
Contact: thexronai@gmail.com
App: CheckMyDrive (Android package `com.xron.checkmydrive`)

## Summary

- CheckMyDrive does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.
- The app has no internet permission. It cannot send anything off your phone.
- There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the app does

CheckMyDrive tests a USB drive (USB stick, SSD or hard disk) that you plug into your phone. It reads the drive, and when you
choose the "full check" it writes test data to the drive (this erases the drive, and the app asks you to confirm first). The result
is a score and a plain-language report.

## Permissions and why

- **USB host access**: used only to talk to the drive you plug in and to run the test. Android asks you to allow access to that device.
- **Notifications and foreground service**: used only to show test progress and to keep a running test alive while your screen is off.
  You can deny notifications; the test still works.
- **Wake lock**: keeps the phone awake during a test.
- The app does **not** request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or any account permission.

## Data on your phone

- **Test history.** Results of your checks (drive model, size, serial number as reported by the drive, scores and findings) are saved in
  the app's private storage on your phone so you can look at them later. They never leave the phone. You can delete one result or all of
  them in the app (History), and uninstalling the app removes everything.
- **Settings.** Your theme choice and language are stored on the phone.
- **Sharing.** If you tap Share, the app creates a picture and an HTML report and hands them to the Android share sheet. You choose
  where they go. The app itself never uploads anything.

## What we do not do

We do not collect data about you or your device. We do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. No third party
receives any data from the app, because the app has no network access.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone.

## Security

Because nothing is transmitted, there is nothing to intercept. Test results stay in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.
